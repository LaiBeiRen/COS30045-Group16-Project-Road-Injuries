# Evaluation plan: dashboard revision

Prepared 4 November 2026. This is a proposed protocol, not participant results. OpenAI Codex assisted this plan.

Test navigation without scrolling, measure interpretation, page/chart filter scope and exact-value retrieval. Adapt the existing Google Form before testing: its earlier tasks describe the previous three-chart prototype.

1. Select 2011 on National picture and report hospitalisations. Expected: 34,033.
2. Change the residence chart to bed days per admission. Explain whether the annual chart also changes. Expected: only that chart changes.
3. On Burden comparison with 2021/all filters, find pedestrian admission and bed-day shares. Expected: about 5.91% and 11.43%. Interpret the gap without claiming risk or clinical severity.
4. Select a road-user mark and identify which page filter changed and its scope.
5. Select a state/year cell and inspect the corresponding trajectory. Explain why raw counts do not establish population risk.
6. On First Nations profiles, select 2011 and explain n.p. in the road-user table. Expected: suppressed/not for publication, not zero.
7. Open a data table, move to another table page, and download its CSV.
8. Use page/chart selectors on a narrow screen. Complete a task using the keyboard and Escape.

Record completion, difficulty, incorrect filter assumptions and misinterpretations. Ask for ratings of navigation, readability, filter clarity and interpretation confidence, with explanations and one improvement. Ask whether the share-gap comparison was understandable and useful.

Participation is voluntary. Avoid unnecessary identifiers and obtain consent before recording. Separate actual responses from expected answers, and document resulting design changes. Do not claim full accessibility from keyboard-only checks.
